The Berkeley Lattice QCD Group was founded in 2010 with LBL LDRD funds of [Wick Haxton](https://physics.berkeley.edu/people/faculty/wick-haxton), with the hiring of André Walker-Loud and Sergey Syritsyn as postdocs.  During this time, in collaboration with Tom Luu and Pavlos Vranas, the [California Lattice (CalLat) Collaboration](https://callat-qcd.github.io) was formed.  André and Sergey left LBNL in 2013 at the time that Thorsten Kurth joined as a postdoc and Evan Berkowitz as an Affiliate postdoc, followed by Amy Nicholson as a postdoc.

In 2015, André returned to LBNL as a Career Track Scientist to lead the lattice QCD effort, working closely with Amy, Evan and Jason Chang who started at LBNL with André's return, followed by Ben Hörz and Aaron Meyer.  The group established itself with several important publications enabled through DOE INCITE Awards.  As the junior members of the group left for other positions and geographically disbursed, and new colleagues joined, the local CalLat effort transitioned to become a key partner of the [CoSMoN Collaboration](https://cosmon-collaboration.github.io) and the BaSc Collaboration.

In 2023, Raúl Briceño joined UC Berkeley and LBNL as a Faculty Scientist, bringing a node of the [ExoHad Collaboration](https://www.exohad.org), additional resources and a stronger connection to UC Berkeley and the group grew significantly in size to what it is today.
